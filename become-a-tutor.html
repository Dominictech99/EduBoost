Tutor Requirements

To maintain high-quality learning experiences, we require every tutor to meet the following standards.

Educational Qualification

Hold at least an OND, HND, Bachelor's degree, or equivalent qualification in your area of expertise.

Subject Knowledge

Demonstrate excellent knowledge of the subjects you intend to teach.

Internet Access

Have a reliable internet connection and a computer or smartphone for online lessons.

Communication Skills

Be able to explain concepts clearly and communicate effectively with students.
